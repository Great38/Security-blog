Physical Security & Threat Assessment

Implementing Defense-in-Depth for Critical Infrastructure

Published September 15, 2026

Securing high-value physical assets requires a multi-layered barrier strategy where no single point of failure can compromise facility integrity. Concentric rings of security ensure early detection, deterrence, delay, and response execution.

  • Perimeter Outer Ring: High-definition thermal surveillance, continuous physical barriers, and structured access control gates.
  • Inner Perimeter Zone: Motion detection corridors, multi-factor credential checkposts, and redundant lighting arrays.
  • Core Critical Area: Anti-passback access protocols, localized alarms, and active Security Operations Center (SOC) monitoring.
